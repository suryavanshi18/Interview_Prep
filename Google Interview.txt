Google interview (end to end). Real questions I got from my friend who recently cracked Google. 

1. Recruiter screen (15–20 min)  
“Why Google?”  
“Current comp, expectations, relocation willingness?”

2. Phone screen 1 – Coding (45–60 min)  
- “Design TinyURL.” (hashing + base62 + collision handling)  
- “Find median from data stream.” (two heaps)  
Follow ups: handle duplicates, negatives, space optimization

3. Phone screen 2 – Coding (45–60 min)  
- “Longest substring without repeating characters.” (sliding window)  
- “Serialize and deserialize binary tree.” (preorder + null markers)  
Follow ups: time/space, edge cases (empty tree, single node)

4. Onsite – Coding Round 1 (45 min)  
- “Word break II.” (DP + backtracking)  
Follow ups: optimize space, return all valid sentences

5. Onsite – Coding Round 2 (45 min)  
- “Alien dictionary.” (topological sort + graph)  
Follow ups: cycle detection, multiple valid orders

6. Onsite – System design (45 min)  
“Design YouTube.”  
Covered:  
- video upload/download (chunking, resumable, GCS)  
- recommendation (collaborative filtering + content-based)  
- search (inverted index, ranking, autocomplete)  
- scale (1B+ DAU, hot videos, CDN, sharding)  
- consistency vs availability for views/counts  
- transcoding pipeline, thumbnails, notifications

7. Onsite – Behavioral / Googliness (45 min)  
“Tell me about a time you disagreed with a senior engineer.”  
“How did you handle a tight deadline with quality tradeoffs?”  
“What’s the hardest bug you debugged in production?”

So if you’re prepping: nail clean, optimal code under time pressure & explain tradeoffs verbally. Google loves candidates who think aloud clearly and show “Googleyness” (ownership & humility).
